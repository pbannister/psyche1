# Feature Index

- `01-site-build.md` — Site Build: generates `site.out/` from `site.in/`.
- `02-project-pages.md` — Project Pages: the standard published page set.
- `03-speech-to-text.md` — Speech to Text: transcribe a captured audio buffer locally.
- `04-text-to-speech.md` — Text to Speech: local Piper synthesis and playback.
- `05-facial-recognition.md` — Facial Recognition: detect and label faces in the video feed.
- `06-voice-recognition.md` — Voice Recognition: local microphone transcription with voice-activity status.
- `07-audio-io.md` — Audio I/O: asynchronous duplex audio and microphone-to-speaker loopback.
- `08-video-capture.md` — Video Capture: background webcam frame capture.
- `09-main-window.md` — Main Window: the PyQt6 desktop shell and application entry point.

- `01-site-build.md` and `02-project-pages.md` are the skeleton's worked
  examples for the publishing toolchain; the remaining features are Psyche1's
  own capabilities.
